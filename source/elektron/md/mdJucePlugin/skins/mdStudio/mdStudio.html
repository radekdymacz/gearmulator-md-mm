Machinedrum OS 1.63 · every edit goes to the emulated machine and is read back

Hardware rules this editor follows (Machinedrum OS 1.63 manual)
  • 16 tracks. Each track holds one machine: TRX, EFM, E12, P-I, GND, CTR, ROM, RAM or MID.
  • Pattern length is 16, 32, 48 or 64 steps. Tempo multiplier is 1X, 2X, 3/4X or 3/2X.
  • A pattern can lock 64 parameters in total, shared by all tracks. A locked parameter can take its own value on every step of that track.
  • Locks live on trigs. A step with no trig cannot hold a lock, and removing a trig removes its locks.
  • Locks work only in EXTENDED mode. In CLASSIC they stay in the pattern but do nothing.
  • Accent is 0–15. Swing is 50–80 %, where 50 % means no swing.
  • Each track has three pages of 8: SYNTHESIS (machine), EFFECTS (AMD AMF EQF EQG FLTF FLTW FLTQ SRR) and ROUTING (DIST VOL PAN DEL REV LFOS LFOD LFOM).
  • 16 LFOs per kit: TRCK, PARAM, SHP1, SHP2 (6 shapes, SHP2 inverted), UPDTE (FREE, TRIG, HOLD), SPD, DEPTH, SHMIX.
  • Master effects (Rhythm Echo, Gate Box, EQ, Dynamix) are saved in the kit, work on the main outputs only and cannot be locked.
  • Mute and trig relations live in the kit (EDIT KIT, RELATE column).
  • UW only: 4 RAM recorders (RAM-R1 to R4) capture the main mix and inputs A/B on a trig. RAM-P1 to P4 play them back. RAM is lost at power-off.
  • UW only: 48 ROM slots keep samples after power-off. ROM and RAM share one DSP memory; less ROM in use means better RAM quality.
  • CTR machines have no sound: CTR-AL moves a parameter on all tracks, CTR-8P holds 8 shortcuts (a trigless trig), CTR-RE/GB/EQ/DX let you lock the master effects.
  • MID machines send NOTE plus two chord notes (N2, N3), LEN, VEL, PB, MW, AT and 5 CCs to external gear.
  • A song has up to 256 rows. Each row sets pattern, start, length, tempo and mutes. 32 songs fit in memory.
