{{APP_NAME}} {{VERSION}}
====================================

Drag each item onto the folder next to it:

  {{APP_NAME}}.app        ->  {{LINK_APPS}}
  {{APP_NAME}}.vst3       ->  {{LINK_VST3}}
  {{APP_NAME}}.component  ->  {{LINK_AU}}

Take only what you use: the app on its own needs no plug-ins, and a DAW
needs only the VST3 or the Audio Unit. Finder asks for an administrator
password for the two plug-in folders. Without one, put the plug-ins in your
own Library instead (Finder > Go > Go to Folder):

  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

Then restart your DAW or ask it to rescan plug-ins. Keep one copy of each
plug-in, not one in /Library and another in ~/Library, or the DAW lists two.

Upgrading from 0.3.1 or earlier: the plug-ins were called {{OLD_NAME}}.vst3
and {{OLD_NAME}}.component then. Delete those from the plug-in folders, or
the DAW lists the editor twice. Projects that used them open with
{{APP_NAME}}: the plug-in is the same one under its new name.

Prefer an installer? Machinedrum-Editor-macOS.pkg / Monomachine-Editor-macOS.pkg
from the same release page puts all three in place for you.

Firmware is not included. On first start the app or plug-in shows the folder
for it:

  ~/Documents/Gearmulator Preview/{{DATA_FOLDER}}/roms/

Copy a {{MACHINE}} firmware image you are entitled to use there, then restart.
Please do not ask anyone, in any channel, for firmware files.

Removing it: delete the three items from where you dropped them. Your
firmware and settings in Documents are not touched.
