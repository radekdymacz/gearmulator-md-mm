{{APP_NAME}} {{VERSION}}
====================================

This disk image holds the files loose, to put in place by hand: there is no
installer in it. The installer is {{MACHINE}}-Editor-macOS.pkg, a separate
download on the same release page (https://mdmm.dev/get/). It puts all three in
place for you and removes the old ones (see "Upgrading" below). Use this disk
image only if you would rather place the files yourself.

Drag each item onto the folder next to it:

  {{APP_NAME}}.app        ->  {{LINK_APPS}}
  {{APP_NAME}}.vst3       ->  {{LINK_VST3}}
  {{APP_NAME}}.component  ->  {{LINK_AU}}

Take only what you use: the app on its own needs no plug-ins, and a DAW
needs only the VST3 or the Audio Unit. Finder asks for an administrator
password for the two plug-in folders. Without one, put the plug-ins in your
own Library instead (Finder > Go > Go to Folder):

  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

Then restart your DAW or ask it to rescan plug-ins. Keep one copy of each
plug-in, not one in /Library and another in ~/Library, or the DAW lists two.

Upgrading from 0.3.1 or earlier: the plug-ins were called {{OLD_NAME}}.vst3
and {{OLD_NAME}}.component then (and the app {{OLD_NAME}}.app, when it came from
the zip). Dragging the new ones in does not remove the old ones, and a DAW then
lists the editor twice. Delete the old ones from every place they are in, in
Finder > Go > Go to Folder:

  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/{{OLD_NAME}}.vst3
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/{{OLD_NAME}}.component
  /Applications/{{OLD_NAME}}.app

and the same three under your own home folder (~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3,
~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components, ~/Applications). Projects that used them
open with {{APP_NAME}}: the plug-in is the same one under its new name. The
installer (.pkg) deletes these for you.

Firmware is not included. On first start the app or plug-in shows the folder
for it:

  ~/Documents/Gearmulator Preview/{{DATA_FOLDER}}/roms/

Copy a {{MACHINE}} firmware image you are entitled to use there, then restart.
Please do not ask anyone, in any channel, for firmware files.

Removing it: delete the three items from where you dropped them (and any
{{OLD_NAME}} ones, see above). Your firmware and settings in Documents are not
touched.
