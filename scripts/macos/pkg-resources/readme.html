Before you start

This installer is not signed yet

There is no Apple Developer ID for this project yet, so macOS warns about the installer. If you are reading this, you already got past that step. If the app itself is blocked the first time you open it:

  1. open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to the message about {{APP_NAME}} (on macOS 15 and later right-click › Open no longer gets past this); or
  2. in Terminal:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/{{APP_NAME}}.app"
    (installed for you only: "$HOME/Applications/{{APP_NAME}}.app")

In your DAW

The plug-ins show up as {{APP_NAME}} by Future Native Audio (VST3 and Audio Unit, instrument). Up to 0.3.1 they were called {{OLD_NAME}}; projects saved with those open with {{APP_NAME}}. Rescan plug-ins or restart the DAW after installing. Install either for all users or for you only, not both: with a copy in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins and another in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins the DAW lists two. Remove the one you do not want.

Where your files live

  • Firmware: ~/Documents/Gearmulator Preview/{{DATA_FOLDER}}/roms/
  • Settings and logs: ~/Documents/Gearmulator Preview/{{DATA_FOLDER}}/ (the editor's settings: config/{{APP_NAME}}.xml) and, for the app, ~/Library/Application Support/{{APP_NAME}}.settings

Removing it

Delete /Applications/{{APP_NAME}}.app, /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/{{APP_NAME}}.vst3 and /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/{{APP_NAME}}.component; if you installed for you only, the same three in ~/Applications and ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. Your firmware and settings in Documents are not touched.
