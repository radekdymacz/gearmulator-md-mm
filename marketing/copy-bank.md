## Replies (comment bank)

- *Windows?* — Not yet. macOS only for now; the source is GPL-3 on GitHub.
- *Where do I get the ROM?* — It has to be your own (MD OS 1.63 / MM OS 1.32B). We don't supply or link firmware.
- *Does it work with my real MD?* — Over MIDI, in beta: tested against the emulator so far. Feedback welcome.
- *Is it Elektron's?* — No, independent and not affiliated; built on joelanders' and The Usual Suspects' open-source emulation.
- *Why free?* — It's GPL software. Pay what you want if it helps you; €0 is fine.
