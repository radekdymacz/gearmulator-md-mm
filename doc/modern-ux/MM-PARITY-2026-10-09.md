# MM parity review, 2026-10-09: what the Machinedrum Editor has that the Monomachine Editor lacks

- Reviewed at `origin/release/0.3.5` (509cdd5f9), read-only. Prior art: [MM-PORT-PLAN.md](MM-PORT-PLAN.md) (the
  2026-10-05 gap list: everything then was ported except the live-recording lock marker). Since then the MD got
  0.3.3-0.3.5: the key map ([DESIGN-keymap.md](DESIGN-keymap.md)), step selection
  ([DESIGN-step-selection.md](DESIGN-step-selection.md)), the step menu, the GLOBAL key and panel, the note map, the
  stream, the version, the SysEx import report.
- **In flight on 0.3.5, not counted here:** the song playhead, the Song page redesign, the PLAY PAT/SONG LCD field and
  the host-tempo TEMPO display (both editors, another agent).
- **Excluded:** the Sampler (MD only, the MM has none).
- Compared: `skins/mdStudio/*` + `skins/shared/*` against the generated `skins/mmStudio/mmMockup.js` (sources
  `mm-mockup/src/*`) and `mmAdapter.js`; `mdDesk/*` against `mmDesk/*` (command and edit tables); `keymap.json` (`md`
  108 entries, `mm` 68); `mdDeskJourneys.js` against `mmJourneys.js`; `mdLib` (shared by both machines, branches on
  `isMonomachine()`); `mdJucePlugin` (shared host, per-machine `mdSessionMd.cpp` / `mdSessionMm.cpp`).
- Applies: **a** = applies to the MM, port it; **b** = does not apply (the hardware differs; manual page cited, MM
  manual OS 1.32 rev H, via [mm-manual-mapping.md](mm-manual-mapping.md)); **c** = unclear, needs a firmware probe or
  a measurement first. **=** = parity (listed where the question was asked). Size: S under a day, M a few days,
  L a week or more (core + page + firmware test).

## 1. Stability and performance

A Windows 10 tester (i9, 32 GB, 0.3.4): the MD standalone runs clean; the MM standalone crackles heavily and its page
lags, at about 8 % CPU. Low CPU with crackle points at stalls (something waits inside the audio callback or the page's
process), not at raw load. Every fix the MD got for audio, CPU or page cost, and where the MM stands:

## 4. Sizes

- Stability: the play-head page cost (M), the MM page-cost harness (S-M), B-034's rest for the MM path (S-M), the
  MM transport-policy probe (L, starts as a measurement). About 2-3 weeks if the scheduling probe turns into a fix.
- Features: GLOBAL panel with channels and CONTROL (L + 2 M + S), selection and its keys, drop-copy, step menu,
  D3, LCD keys (K7: L + 3 M + 3 S), keyboard-view entries (S), five journeys (S each), recLock (probe + M). About
  4-5 weeks.
- Reverse: MD black keys (S), three MD journeys (S).

## 5. Suggested order

1. **0.3.6, stability first:** measure the MM transport (rt-check with wall time and backpressure counts) on the
   tester's symptom; port the play-head page fix; the MM page-cost harness; the B-034 rest (both machines).
2. **0.3.6, small and high value:** GLOBAL key + panel with MIDI channels (the B-026 dead end), then CONTROL IN / OUT
   and the active slot; `mm-lib-syx-import-mute`; the keyboard-view entries.
3. **0.3.7:** K7 in one pass: selection core, ⌘ select on the roll, keys, step menu, D3, LCD keys, drop-copy, with
   `mm-seq-select-copy-paste` and `mm-seq-step-menu`.
4. Later: the recLock probe; MD black keys; the remaining journeys.
