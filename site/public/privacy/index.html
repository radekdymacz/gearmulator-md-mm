Privacy

Privacy

This site sets no cookies, runs no tracking or analytics, and loads no third-party scripts. There are no accounts.

This browser

If you switch to the dark theme, your browser keeps that choice under the name mdmm.theme in its local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) to run and protect the service.

Downloads

The installers are hosted on GitHub, as GitHub Releases. When you download one, GitHub sees your IP address and the request, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive that information. The same applies if you open the source repository or the releases page.

Update check in the editors

At most once a day the editors fetch https://mdmm.dev/latest.json to see whether a newer version exists — a plain request with no cookies, query or identifiers, which you can turn off in the editor's menu (Updates › Check Daily) — and an update you choose to install comes from GitHub, as above.

Purchases

If you choose to pay, the purchase is processed by Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record, under its own privacy policy. Pay opens Lemon Squeezy checkout and sends it three custom fields: the editor you chose, your operating system and the amount you suggested. We receive the order details Lemon Squeezy shares with sellers.
