Install

The app and the plug-ins are not signed by Apple

  1. App: unzip it and move it to Applications.
  2. First launch: right-click (or Control-click) the app, choose Open, then Open again. If macOS still refuses, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Plug-in: copy the .vst3 bundle to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ and the .component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/, then rescan in your DAW.
  4. If the DAW will not load it: macOS may have quarantined the download. In Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/<name>.vst3 (and the same for the .component).
Bring your own ROM

Add your firmware image

  1. Have your own firmware image ready: Machinedrum OS 1.63 or Monomachine OS 1.32B, a full 8 MiB .bin. It is not included, and we cannot provide it.
  2. Open the editor once. It shows NO ROM and a key that opens its ROM folder:
    ~/Documents/Gearmulator Preview/Machinedrum/roms/ or …/Monomachine/roms/
  3. Put the image in that folder.
  4. Quit and reopen the app or plug-in. The editor checks the image's fingerprint, boots the OS and reads the machine. That takes 10–15 seconds.
