FAQ

Before you download.

Do I need a ROM?

Yes, your own: Machinedrum OS 1.63 or Monomachine OS 1.32B, a full 8 MiB image. No firmware is included and we can't supply it.

How do I add the ROM?

Open the editor. The start-up card asks for your firmware: click it, choose the .bin (or a .zip with it) in the file chooser, and the editor checks it and copies it into its ROM folder. Drag and drop is not supported. Steps, keys and DAW use are in the user guide.

Does it work with my real machine?

In beta. HW MIDI drives a real Machinedrum or Monomachine over MIDI, but so far it has only been tested against the emulator.

Is it really free?

Yes, free software under the GPL-3. At download you can name your price; €0 is fine.

How do I uninstall it?

Delete the bundles the installer put in place. For the Machinedrum Editor: Machinedrum Editor.app in /Applications, Machinedrum Editor.vst3 in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and Machinedrum Editor.component in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components. For the Monomachine Editor: the same with Monomachine Editor.app, Monomachine Editor.vst3 and Monomachine Editor.component. If you installed for yourself only, look in ~/Applications and ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. Your ROM and settings stay where they are.

My DAW will not load the AU

macOS may have quarantined the unsigned component. In Terminal run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Machinedrum Editor.component" (use Monomachine Editor.component for the Monomachine, and ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components if you installed for yourself only), then rescan plug-ins in your DAW.

Known limits
  • Tested on macOS, Apple silicon (M4 Pro). A Windows x64 build (app and VST3) is available but not tested yet. Linux is coming.
  • Not notarised by Apple. On macOS 15 and later the first install is blocked: open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about the installer and choose Open Anyway (then confirm). Right-click → Open no longer works for installers.
  • About half a CPU core per instance.
  • Over MIDI: no sample transfers; live recording and chains need the emulator.
  • Alpha software: keep backups.
