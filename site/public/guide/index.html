User guide

Everything a first-time user needs for the Machinedrum Editor and the Monomachine Editor, version 0.3.1. Tested on macOS (Intel and Apple silicon builds). A Windows x64 build is available but not tested. Linux is coming.

Getting started

  1. Download the installer for your machine. Each installer holds the standalone app, the VST3 and the AU.
  2. Double-click the .pkg. It is not notarised by Apple, so macOS 15 and later blocks it. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about the installer, click Open Anyway and confirm with your password or Touch ID. Right-click → Open no longer works for installers.
  3. At Destination Select choose Install for all users (needs an administrator) or Install for me only. The app goes to /Applications or ~/Applications. The plug-ins go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins or ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins.
  4. Open Machinedrum Editor or Monomachine Editor from Applications. Next, add your ROM.

Add your ROM

The editors run the machine's real firmware. You bring your own: Machinedrum OS 1.63 or Monomachine SFX-60 OS 1.32B, an 8 MiB image. No firmware is included and we cannot supply it.

  1. Open the editor. With no ROM there is no error pop-up. A start-up card says Machinedrum firmware needed (or Monomachine).
  2. Click the large area on the card: Choose your Machinedrum OS 1.63 ROM (.bin or .zip, 8 MB) (for the Monomachine, Choose your Monomachine SFX-60 OS 1.32B ROM). Under it, Click here to choose the file. The file chooser opens. Pick the .bin, or a .zip that holds it. Drag and drop is not supported.
  3. The editor checks the file and copies it into its ROM folder: on macOS ~/Documents/Gearmulator Preview/Machinedrum/roms/, on Windows Documents\Gearmulator Preview\Machinedrum\roms\ (or the Monomachine folder). The firmware stays on your computer and is sent nowhere. The machine then starts, which takes 10–15 seconds.

You can also copy the image into that folder yourself. On the card, the links Show the ROM folder and Check again open the folder and look again. A file that is the wrong OS or a damaged dump is refused, with the reason on the card.

To replace or remove the firmware later, use LOAD ROM in the engine menu. Your project stays. A session that starts without a ROM keeps your saved project, and in a DAW it keeps the automation and the editor setup until the ROM arrives.

Emulator or real machine

The engine menu in the editor's header chooses what the editor drives.

  • EMU OS 1.63 (Monomachine: EMU OS 1.32B), the default. The real firmware runs inside the editor. It needs the ROM above. Nothing else is needed.
  • HW MIDI (beta). The editor drives your own machine over MIDI. Choose its MIDI in and out in AUDIO/MIDI… in the same menu (also the , key). Until the machine answers, the editor dims and a card offers AUDIO / MIDI or a return to the emulator. It has so far only been tested against the emulator, not a real machine.

The same menu also has LOAD ROM and, in the Machinedrum Editor, GLOBAL….

Over HW MIDI, some controls are disabled because the machine has no MIDI message for them. The editor says why. Use the standalone app for HW MIDI. In most DAWs the plug-in has no MIDI output to the machine, so HW MIDI is disabled there.

In a DAW

After the installer, rescan plug-ins in your DAW. The plug-ins are called Gearmulator MD and Gearmulator MM, as VST3 and AU instruments. They follow the DAW's tempo and transport. The plug-in window shows the same editor, and the same start-up card if the ROM is missing.

If a DAW will not load the AU, macOS may have quarantined it. In Terminal run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Gearmulator MD.component"

Use Gearmulator MM.component for the Monomachine, and ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components if you installed for yourself only. Then rescan plug-ins.

Workspaces

Each editor has five workspaces. Switch with the tabs, or with the keys 1 to 5.

Machinedrum Editor

1 SequenceThe step grid for all 16 tracks, with trigs, accents, slides and drawn parameter locks.
2 SoundOne track: its machine, effects, routing and LFO, grouped by function.
3 MixA strip per track (level, pan, drive, sends, output, mute and solo) and the master effects.
4 SamplerSample slots as waveform tiles, loading and auditioning WAV or AIFF files, and a RAM view of steps.
5 SongThe arrangement: add patterns to the song, chain them and edit rows.

Monomachine Editor

1 SequenceA piano roll with slide and swing rows, and a lock lane. Paint notes by dragging.
2 SoundOne track's sound, grouped by function.
3 MixChannel strips with pan, amp volume, distortion and delay send, one level fader, and the routing into the buses.
4 PerformKeyboard modes, multi trig, multi map, multi envelope, assign and mutes.
5 SongThe arrangement: pick a song, add and edit rows.

More to know

  • GEN (Sequence): a rhythm generator per track. R rolls a new variation, ⌥ R does every track. One undo step.
  • MUTATE (Sound): vary a sound at random, then undo it.
  • Import SysEx… and Export SysEx, in the kit and pattern libraries: open a .syx and choose what to import, or save a backup.
  • In a library, drag a kit (or pattern) slot onto another to copy it.
  • Set up sampling (Machinedrum, Sampler): one card that sets up the recorder and the player.
  • Transpose (Monomachine, Perform): a clickable one-octave keyboard.

Known limits

  • A Machinedrum kit that has a CTR-AL machine cannot be edited yet.
  • Over HW MIDI you can send a WAV or AIFF to a sample slot when the MIDI port can send SysEx. Samples cannot be read back, so there are no waveforms and no audition. Live recording and chains need the emulator.

Keyboard shortcuts

Press ? in an editor for its own list, which is built from the same key map. ⌘ is Command, ⌥ is Option, ⇧ is Shift. A plain letter acts on the selected track. ⌥ means all tracks. MD means the Machinedrum Editor only, MM the Monomachine Editor only.

Playing

A S D F G H J K LPlay the selected track on the white keys C D E F G A B C D, from any workspace. On the Machinedrum, ROM and RAM-P machines are pitched and others play at their own pitch. On the Monomachine they send real notes on the track's MIDI channel. While recording, the keys record.
W E T Y U O PMM: the black keys above them, C♯ D♯ F♯ G♯ A♯ C♯ D♯.
Z XOctave down and up. MD: −2 to +2. MM: −3 to +3.
C VVelocity down and up: 20, 40, 60, 80, 100, 127.

Transport

SpacePlay or stop.
⌥ SpaceLive recording (RECORD and PLAY). MM: press again to stop recording.
T or BTap tempo, from the average of the last taps. MD: both keys. MM: B only, because T plays F♯.

Selected track

↑ ↓Select the previous or next track. A focused value keeps the arrows for itself.
MMute or unmute it.
RRandomise it. On Sound, a fresh random sound (mutate). Elsewhere, a new rhythm-generator variation: a new seed, or random hits and rotation.
⌥ ← →Sequence: rotate the track one step earlier or later, wrapping at its length. One undo step while ⌥ stays down.

All tracks

⌥ MMute every track. When none is audible, unmute every track.
0Unmute and unsolo every track.
⌥ RRandomise every track. On Sound, the whole kit (MM: every synth track).
⌥ DeleteSequence: clear the whole pattern.

Workspaces, sequence and song

1 to 5Switch workspace, in the order of the tables above.
[ ]Previous or next page of 16 steps (MD: also on Sampler).
Delete or BackspaceSequence: clear the selected steps (MM: the page shown of the selected track). Song: delete the row.
EscSequence: unmark the tracks marked for paste.
← →Song: previous or next row.

Values

↑ ↓ ← →On a focused value, tempo or bar: one step. Hold ⇧ for ten, or a fine step on tempo.

Anywhere

⌘ ZUndo.
⌘ ⇧ Z or ⌘ YRedo.
⌘ C and ⌘ VCopy and paste: a track page, a sound, a song row. On the Monomachine, also a Perform assign.
,AUDIO / MIDI settings.
?The editor's own list of keys. ? or Esc closes it.
EscClose the open dialog or panel.

Kit library and pattern chooser

Enter or SpaceOn the KIT or pattern field: open the library or chooser.
Arrow keysMove. Kits are not loaded while you move.
EnterKits: load (a click loads too). Patterns: queue.
A to HPatterns: jump to a bank.
F2Kits: rename (or double-click).
DeleteClear the slot.
⌘ C, ⌘ V, ⌘ ZCopy, paste, and undo a paste, clear or rename.
EscClose.

Mouse with a key held

⇧ click a stepMD: accent. MM: a chord note on the step.
⌥ click a stepMD: slide. MM: delete a note, or a NOTE OFF on an empty step.
⌘ click a stepMD: every second step from there to the end on (off, from a trig). With ⇧, every fourth step. One undo step. MM: the same in the piano roll.
Drag in the MM piano rollPress an empty cell and drag to paint that note across the empty steps. ⌥ drag erases. One drag is one undo step.
⇧ drag a lock laneA straight ramp from the press to the release.
⌥ drag a lock laneErase locks. ⌥ click the lane's clear key to clear every lock of the track.
Wheel on a stepMove its lock in the lane's parameter, 4 a notch (⇧: 1).
⇧ click a track headerMark the track for paste. ⌘ V then pastes into every marked track.
⌥ drag a valueControl All: move that value on every track.
⌥ click CLR or RClear the whole pattern, or randomise every track.
⇧ click an M keyPrepare that track's mute. It applies when you let go of ⇧.
Drag across M or S keysMute (or solo) many tracks at once, as the first key became.

Uninstall

Delete the three bundles of each editor. Your ROM and your settings are not touched.

AppMachinedrum Editor.app and Monomachine Editor.app in /Applications (or ~/Applications)
VST3Gearmulator MD.vst3 and Gearmulator MM.vst3 in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 (or ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3)
AUGearmulator MD.component and Gearmulator MM.component in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components (or ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components)
